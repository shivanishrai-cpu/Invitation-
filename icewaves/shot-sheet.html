Catch the Wave

23.5 s 9:16 vertical 120 BPM · 1 beat = 0.5 s 1 phone · shop counter
The idea

A paper wave in the logo's blue does every transition

A paper wave sweeps across an empty glass and leaves the dark sauce swirl behind. Every step after that (blend, pour, kunafa) is another wave, until both shakes stand together and the wave washes back to the empty glass.

  • The wave is the logo.
  • The sauce streak inside the glass already curls like a wave.
  • "A new wave just hit Uliyathaduka" is a launch line and a play on the brand name.

Technique

The four hard shots

1 · Wave hook (hidden cut)

  1. Phone on the tripod. Long-press the glass to lock focus and exposure.
  2. Clip A: empty glass, then sweep the paper wave in until it covers the lens. Hold 1 s.
  3. Stop recording. Don't touch the phone.
  4. Swap in the glass that already has the sauce, on the same tape mark.
  5. Clip B: start with the wave covering the lens, then pull it away.
  6. In the edit, cut while the frame is fully blue. Use the same method for the loop at the end.

Wave prop: blue chart paper cut into a curl copied from the logo's wave, with a white paper foam edge glued on, taped to a ruler. Hold it about 10 cm from the lens so it fills the frame.

2 · Sauce spiral in the glass

  1. Chill the glass in the freezer for 10 minutes so the sauce grips the wall.
  2. Tilt the glass to about 45°.
  3. Touch the squeeze bottle's nozzle to the inside wall near the rim.
  4. Squeeze gently while you turn the glass one full turn and move the nozzle down to the base.
  5. Wipe condensation off the outside before filming.
  6. Do 3–4 takes. Keep the cleanest spiral.

3 · The pour

  1. Use a jug with a spout.
  2. Pour down the centre of the glass, not against the wall, so the sauce streak stays visible.
  3. Pour slowly and steadily from about 15 cm above the rim.
  4. Phone at glass height, side-on, 1080p at 120fps. Slow it down in the edit.
  5. Film both pours from the exact same spot so they match.

4 · Kunafa on top

  1. Lay the golden kunafa strands on with your fingers in a ring. Don't dump them.
  2. Then sprinkle chopped pistachios, or crumble a biscuit, from about 10 cm.
  3. Keep the biscuit's printed face turned down so no logo shows.
  4. Film at real speed, 45° down, close.
Setup

Light, paper, phone, sound, edit

Lighting

  • Switch off the overhead tube lights. Mixed light turns cream paper green-grey.
  • Glass about 1 m from a window, window to one side and a little behind. Light through the glass makes the sauce and faceted base glow.
  • Direct sun? Tape baking paper over the window.
  • Hold white chart paper on the other side, just out of frame, to fill shadows.

Background and surface

  • Curve one chart-paper sheet from the wall onto the counter so there's no horizon line.
  • Green for pistachio, mustard for Biscoff, cream for the finale.
  • Hold the posters next to the paper at the shop and pick the closest shade.
  • Tape small marks where each glass stands.

Phone settings

  • Vertical 9:16, on a tripod or wedged between books.
  • Normal shots: 4K, 30fps. Pours: 1080p, 120fps.
  • 1x lens. The wide lens bends the glass.
  • Long-press to lock focus and exposure (iPhone AE/AF Lock, Android Pro mode).
  • Exposure down about −0.3 to −0.7 so cream doesn't blow out.
  • Wipe the lens. Airplane mode on. Grid on.

Sound

  • Record real sounds with the phone about 15 cm away, fans off: blender, pour, kunafa crunch, glass tap, and the paper wave's swoosh.
  • Music: an upbeat 110–120 BPM track from Instagram's in-app licensed library. On a business account, use the Meta Sound Collection.
  • Keep the real sounds louder than the music on the blender, pour and crunch.

Edit rhythm

  • Edit in CapCut or Instagram Edits on a 120 BPM grid: one beat is 0.5 s, one bar of 4 beats is 2 s.
  • Every hard cut lands on a beat.
  • The three wave wipes (11.0, 18.5, 22.5 s) land on the start of a bar.
  • The hook reveal hits the first downbeat at 1.5 s.
  • Keep each text on screen at least 1.5 s.

Prop list

  • 3 tall faceted glasses (one empty, two for shakes)
  • Squeeze bottle of the dark sauce
  • Blender, spouted jug
  • Kunafa strands, chopped pistachios, Biscoff biscuits
  • Chart paper: green, mustard, cream, blue, white
  • Ruler, tape, scissors, glue
  • Logo printed on card (or the original logo file for the edit)
  • Tripod or books, cloth for wiping glasses
